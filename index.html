ABOUT THE PLATFORM

What is Jharkhand Innovation Connect (JIC)?

JIC is a state-wide innovation governance system designed under Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026) Problem Statement #26043 to solve the disconnect between local governance challenges, higher education research, and public needs.

Our Vision

To transform every unsolved civic, agricultural, and industrial problem in Jharkhand into an academic capstone, patent, or funded startup solution.

AI-Powered Problem Triage

Automatic duplicate clustering, multilingual NLP translation (Hindi, Santhali, Mundari, English), and smart capability-matching to university engineering departments.

Academic & NEP Integration

Students and faculty earn certified UGC Academic Bank of Credits (ABC), university project marks, and state-backed incubation seed grants.

The Triple Helix Collaboration Framework

How JIC synchronizes four key stakeholders seamlessly:

1. CITIZENS

Grassroots Sourcing

Post real problems from wards & panchayats with GPS coordinates, photos, and urgency tags.

2. UNIVERSITIES

R&D & Solution Engineering

BIT Mesra, NIT Jamshedpur, IIT ISM students build scalable IoT, biochemical, and software solutions.

3. DISTRICT MAGISTRATES

Validation & Deployment

DC & BDO offices review, verify ground reality, allocate municipal funds, and approve field deployments.

4. INDUSTRY CSR

Funding & Scaling

Tata Steel, SAIL, BCCL adopt verified challenges with tax-deductible CSR capital for commercialization.

END-TO-END PIPELINE

How Problems Become Solutions

A transparent 4-stage journey from grassroots report to field implementation.

01

Report & Geotag

Citizen or Panchayat representative uploads problem details with photos, audio notes, and automatic GPS location.

02

AI Triage & Verification

Platform AI groups duplicate issues, scores severity, and sends verified briefs to the concerned District Magistrate.

03

University Capstone

Academic teams at BIT Mesra / NIT claim the challenge, build prototypes, and track sprints via the collaborative Kanban workspace.

04

Field Deployment & CSR

District Administration evaluates working prototype on-site, releases deployment funds, and closes the grievance.
