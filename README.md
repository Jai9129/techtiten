## 🌟 Pitch Deck Slide Modules (00 to 12) Implemented

This web application integrates the entire 12-slide SIH 2026 pitch deck as interactive, live modules alongside a fully functional crowdsourcing prototype:

- **Slide 00 (Cover)**: Title, Theme, and 5 Stakeholders (Citizens, Universities, Industry, AI Engine, Government).
- **Slide 01/12 (The Challenge)**: *"What Is The Problem?"* — 5 key points and Disconnected Triangle diagram (Citizens, Universities, Industry with "No Link").
- **Slide 02/12 (Our Approach)**: *"What Is Our Solution?"* — Platform overview and 5-stakeholder Hub-and-Spoke diagram.
- **Slide 03/12 (Citizen Participation)**: *"What Can Citizens Do?"* — 4-step horizontal action flow:
  1. *Report a Problem*
  2. *Tag & Track*
  3. *Support & Vote*
  4. *Get Notified*
- **Slide 04/12 (Intelligent Automation)**: *"What Does AI Do?"* — 5 bullets + 4-stage AI processing pipeline:
  `Raw Reports` &rarr; `Classify & De-duplicate` &rarr; `Match & Prioritise` &rarr; `Insights & Reports`.
- **Slide 05/12 (Academic Role)**: *"What Do Universities Do?"* — 5 bullets + 4-step vertical flow:
  `Pick a Matched Problem` &darr; `Assign Student Team` &darr; `Research & Prototype` &darr; `Partner with Industry`.
- **Slide 06/12 (Industry Partnership)**: *"What Is The Role Of Industry?"* — 2x2 grid:
  `Fund Promising Ideas`, `Provide Technical Mentorship`, `Pilot & Scale Solutions`, `Co-Create with Campuses` (plus interactive CSR adoption & calculator).
- **Slide 07/12 (Progress Tracking)**: *"How Do We Track The Project?"* — 5 bullets + 5-stage interactive Kanban board:
  `Reported` &rarr; `Verified` &rarr; `In Research` &rarr; `Piloting` &rarr; `Resolved`.
- **Slide 08/12 (Administration)**: *"What Does The Government Dashboard Do?"* — 5 bullets + Live Command Console mockup with stat tiles (`1,240 Reported`, `318 In Progress`, `96 Resolved`) and animated district/sector charts.
- **Slide 09/12 (Differentiators)**: *"What Is Innovative About Our Idea?"* — 2x2 grid:
  `One Platform, Four Stakeholders`, `AI-Powered Matching`, `NEP 2020 Aligned`, `Transparent & Trackable`.
- **Slide 10/12 (Tech Stack)**: *"Technologies We Proposed"* — Layered architecture:
  Frontend (React/React Native), Backend (Node.js/Django), AI/ML (NLP), Database (PostgreSQL/GIS), Cloud & Hosting, and Security.
- **Slide 11/12 (Feasibility)**: *"Why Is It Feasible?"* — 2x2 grid:
  `Proven, Available Technology`, `Willing Stakeholders`, `Low Operating Cost`, `Simple Phased Rollout`.
- **Slide 12/12 (Outcomes)**: *"What Impact Will It Create?"* — 4 Stat cards (`1000s Local problems solved`, `100+ Teams`, `Faster Response`, `Full Transparency`) and strategic governance outcomes.

## 💻 Working Prototype Features

1. **Compact 1-Line Header (52px)**: Team Techtiten branding, SIH 26043 badge, smooth slide jump links, and Post Problem CTA.
2. **Citizen Problem Submission Modal**: Title, district, sector, description, urgency, regional voice note simulation, and GPS geo-tagging.
3. **HEI Capstone Claim Modal**: University selection (BIT Mesra, IIT ISM, NIT JSR, etc.), team lead, faculty guide, and NEP 2020 credit allocation.
4. **CSR Prototype Adoption & Calculator**: Dynamic live slider for estimating village footprint, student researchers supported, and citizen impact.
5. **Interactive Challenges Feed**: Instant search, district dropdown (all 24 Jharkhand districts), status filter, sector pills, and live upvoting.

## 🎨 Design System

- **Pure Dark Theme**: Clean `#0B0F17` background, `#121826` cards, `#182234` sub-boxes, and `#F8FAFC` high-contrast typography.
- **No Background Blur Glare**: Crisp, casual, modern enterprise aesthetics without distracting light blobs.
- **Zero Block Logo**: 100% pure lightweight SVG icons.
- **Ultra-Compact**: Sleek padding, zero horizontal scrollbars, and high information density.
